A little about me

Statistics in the classroom and beyond.

I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Mathematics & Statistics at Boston University. My teaching spans introductory statistics for students in the sciences through graduate-level statistical bioinformatics and design of experiments.

My research and collaborations center on practical statistical questions arising in genomics, toxicology, biomedical research, and clinical studies. I enjoy working across disciplines to connect sound statistical methods with substantive scientific questions.

Previously, I held teaching positions at the University of Wisconsin–Madison and Texas A&M University, and research positions at the Texas A&M Center for Environmental Health Research (TiCER) and the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai. I was also involved in the Biomedical Informatics and Behavioral Sciences (BIBS) Summer Research Program at the Texas A&M School of Dentistry (September 2022–August 2025). I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Maryland, Baltimore County.

Consulting & collaborative research

Good questions deserve careful analysis.

I work on statistical methods and interdisciplinary analyses motivated by biomedical and scientific data. Areas of experience include:

01 · Biomedical collaboration

Statistical genomics & bioinformatics

Analysis of high-throughput genomic and methylation data, biomarker discovery, and statistical approaches to cancer-related research.

02 · Environmental health

Toxicology & health research

Collaborative data analysis in toxicology, including studies of biological variability, environmental exposures, and disease outcomes.

03 · Study design

Clinical trials & experiments

Design and analysis of experiments, including methodological interests in multiregional clinical trials and equivalence assessment.

04 · Statistical methodology

High-dimensional data analysis

Variable selection, sufficient dimension reduction, predictive modeling, and statistical methods for complex data.

For potential teaching, statistical consulting, or collaborative research inquiries, please get in touch by email.
